Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Rosina Cafe ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users ("User", "you") of the Rosina Cafe mobile application ("App") and website.

1. Information We Collect

To facilitate your food ordering and preparation process inside our cafe, we collect certain limited personal information when you place a takeaway or dine-in order. This includes:

  • Customer Name: Used to label and identify your order.
  • Phone Number: Used solely to contact you regarding your order status or in case of any issues with your meal.
  • Order Details: The selected dishes, total price, and chosen dining option (Eat In or Takeaway).

2. How Your Data is Secured and Stored

All order and customer data is securely transmitted and stored in our cloud-based database powered by Google Firebase Firestore. We implement industry-standard secure data storage practices to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your personal information.

3. Sharing Your Personal Information

We strictly do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal identification information with any third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or outside partners. Your information is only shared internally with our kitchen staff to prepare and complete your active order.

4. Third-Party Services

Our application integrates specific, reliable services from Google Firebase to ensure stability and real-time operations:

  • Firebase Firestore: Used for secure menu retrieval and order database storage.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to send you automated local system notifications when your order status updates (e.g., when your meal is ready).
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Used to anonymously log system crash reports to help us maintain app stability.

5. Your Data Rights

Since we do not require account registration, we do not maintain long-term personal profiles. However, you have the full right to request the deletion of your historical phone orders or contact references from our Firestore database at any time by contacting us directly.
