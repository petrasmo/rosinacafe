Rosina Cafe: A Culinary Gem in South Ockendon

Nestled along London Road in South Ockendon, England, Rosina Cafe stands as a cherished culinary gem, offering a delightful fusion of British and Galician flavors in a warm, welcoming atmosphere. With a reputation for hearty meals, generous portions, and friendly service, it's no wonder that both locals and visitors consider Rosina Cafe a must-visit destination.

Owned and operated by Yunus Kosar, Rosina Cafe has been serving the community with a diverse menu that caters to various tastes and dietary preferences. From traditional English breakfasts (such as bacon, sausage, eggs, and rolls) to Galician-inspired specialties, the cafe offers a unique dining experience that reflects its commitment to quality, cleanliness, and authenticity.

Diverse Menu & Flavors

Enjoy classic British dishes like bacon, sausages & egg rolls alongside Galician-inspired specialties. Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options are always available.

Exceptional Service

Praised for generous portions, immaculate cleanliness, and friendly, attentive staff under the dedicated management of Yunus Kosar.
